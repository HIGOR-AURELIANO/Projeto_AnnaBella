
## 2025-05-14 - Navigation Context and Keyboard Accessibility
**Learning:** Enhancing navigation menus with visual (`.active`) and programmatic (`aria-current="page"`) feedback significantly improves orientation for all users. Additionally, global `:focus-visible` styles are essential for keyboard users who rely on clear focus indicators that do not distract mouse users.
**Action:** Always implement `aria-current` on navigation links and ensure high-visibility focus indicators using `:focus-visible` to balance accessibility and aesthetic requirements. Ensure workspace is cleaned of verification artifacts before submission.
